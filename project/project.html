EcoFriendly Homes Website Project Site Plan

Site Name

EcoFriendly Homes

EcoFriendly Homes is a luxury short-let serviced apartment company located in Abuja, Nigeria. The name was selected because it reflects the company's focus on providing comfortable, modern, environmentally conscious accommodation equipped with smart home technology and premium hospitality services.

Optional Domain: ecofriendlyhomes.ng

Site Purpose

The website will showcase EcoFriendly Homes' serviced apartments, allowing visitors to explore available accommodations, amenities, apartment galleries, pricing, locations, and booking information. The website will also provide contact information and highlight the company's commitment to luxury, comfort, sustainability, and smart living.

Target Audience

  • Business travelers
  • Tourists visiting Abuja
  • Families
  • Corporate organizations
  • Vacation guests
  • Expatriates

Scenarios

  • Which serviced apartment is available for my preferred travel dates, and what amenities are included?
  • How much does it cost to stay at EcoFriendly Homes, and how can I make a reservation?
  • Does the apartment provide Wi-Fi, backup power, housekeeping, and smart security systems?

Color Scheme

Color Hex Code Usage
Forest Green #2E7D32 Header, navigation, buttons, headings, footer
Sky Blue #4FC3F7 Links, icons, call-to-action buttons, highlights
White #FFFFFF Page background and content sections
Dark Gray #333333 Body text and paragraphs

Typography

  • Poppins — Headings, navigation menu, hero section, buttons.
  • Roboto — Body text, descriptions, forms, and general content.

Reason for Choosing this Project

I selected EcoFriendly Homes because my company, ZectaBridge, recently completed the finishing work, smart systems integration, and electrical installations for one of their serviced apartment locations in Abuja. Since the company currently does not have an official website, this project gives me the opportunity to design a professional website prototype that showcases their apartments and services while demonstrating the web development skills I have gained throughout this course.
